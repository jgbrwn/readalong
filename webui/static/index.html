ADMINISTRATION

Admin settings

Manage accounts and shared Readalong behavior.

Accounts

Manage account access. Regular book/media access stays owner-scoped; cross-account copies use the audited Books tools.

Cover artwork model

Readalong generates real cover images through OpenRouter’s Images API. The prompt uses the book’s title, author, verified publication year, and—if enabled—a short EPUB description. Audio, chapters, account details, and source URLs are never sent.

Each generated cover is a billable OpenRouter image request. The OpenRouter key stays on the server; set it as OPENROUTER_API_KEY in the private .env file.

Transfer or clone a book

Only complete, idle books can be copied. Clones leave the original in place; transfers move it. The recipient starts with fresh reading progress. No transcription is rerun.

Audio and processed files are copied independently (no shared hard links), up to 8 GiB per operation. This can take a while.

FRESH TRANSCRIPT

Re-transcribe this book?

Readalong will send the saved audio to Groq again. This uses your configured transcription quota and may take a while.

Your current transcript, audio, EPUB, and reading progress stay available until the fresh transcript and alignment (when applicable) finish successfully. If transcription fails or hits a rate limit, the current version is kept.
